Blast or Bust
Module 2: Obstacle Generation

When the Referee needs an immediate challenge for the party, roll one die of each polyhedral tier (1d4, 1d6, 1d8, 1d10, 1d12, and 1d20) in a single throw. In seconds, this generates the obstacle's Difficulty Threshold, evocative form, required Attributes, retaliatory Counterattack, and Health Pool.

Obstacle Health Pool (1d20) Formula: 10 + 1d20

Roll 1d20 and add 10 to establish current Obstacle HP (Range: 11 to 30 HP). Physical obstacles track Physical HP damage; environmental and psychic impasses deplete the pool through Mental actions.

Instant Roll Example

Dice Roll: 1d4 [2: 17+] • 1d6 [5: Dusted] • 1d8 [2: Guardian] • 1d10 [7: COS + WIT] • 1d12 [8: Disarm] • 1d20 [14: 24 HP].
Narrative Translation: A Dusted Guardian (ancient stone colossus half-buried in the white dunes). Hard Threshold 17+ with 24 HP. To defeat it, characters must endure its toxic choking plumes (COS) or outmaneuver its blind swings (WIT). If a character Busts, its pneumatic sweep knocks their weapon away!

The Complication & Counterattack Matrix (1d12)

When a character Busts against the obstacle, it inflicts this lethal reaction:

1d12 Retaliatory Effect / Hazardous Consequence
1Accumulated Trauma: Deals 1d4 damage for each time the character was already damaged by this obstacle.
2Gear Shatter: Deals 0 damage, but completely destroys an item of the character’s choice.
3Violent Tumble: Knocks the character prone (must spend an action or roll AGI to stand).
4Psychic Terror: Inflicts 1d10 Mental Damage (MP) directly to composure.
5Pressure Relief: Step down the Tension Die by one tier (e.g., d8 → d6).
6Vampiric Drain: Inflicts 1d6 damage and the obstacle heals that same amount of HP.
7Overwhelming Dread: All characters' difficulty thresholds rise by one step for the scene.
8Weapon Snatch: Knocks a weapon or special item from grasp, requiring an action to retrieve.
9Ash Blinding: Character is blinded for 1d4 rounds (all checks suffer disadvantage).
10Mental Lock: Forces all opponents to use exclusively Mental attacks on their next turn.
11Mitosis: The obstacle instantly duplicates itself (sharing identical current HP).
12Miraculous Whiff: Nothing happens; the blow strikes empty sand.

The Escalation Engine

Tension is physical. It sits in the center of the table, creeping ever closer to cataclysm with every broken gamble.

The Tension Die Rule Central Table Clock

When introducing this module, the Referee places a d4 at the center of the table designated as the Tension Die.

  • Escalation on Bust: Each time a player Busts (exceeds 21 on a sequential push-your-luck chain), step up the Tension Die by one tier:
Calm (d4) → → → → → Cataclysm (d20)
  • Climax & Reset: At the conclusion of each scene, the Referee rolls the accumulated Tension Die, consults the Wasteland Threat Table below, resolves the threat, and resets the Tension Die back to d4.
  • Lowering the Die: Players can lower the Tension Die by one step by impressing the narrator with stunning roleplay or profound narrative sacrifice (at the Referee's sole discretion).
Roll Threat Level Threat Encounter (Sample Manifestations) Referee Narrative Directive & Stakes
1–5 Safe Safe haven, abandoned cistern, minor tactical advantage, or vital ancient intel. Allows the story to move forward; players catch their breath and consolidate plans.
6–7 Minor Corrosive sand-lice, rusted hinges, blinding squall, or terrain slowing element. Spices up the wasteland march; requires party cooperation to devise a smart solution.
8–9 Moderate Active opposing obstacles: wasteland thugs, scavenger ambush, or a hungry pack of saur-jackals. A genuine battle or challenge; excellent opportunity for characters to shine and gain milestone XP.
10–11 Severe Life-threatening danger: colossal biomechanical terror, villain confrontation, or veteran bounty hunter band. Do not underestimate. A character could be severely maimed, crippled, or lose a cherished relic.
12–19 Extreme Supernatural cataclysm: awakening avatar of a minor evil god, sworn Nemesis squad, inescapable run-away hazard. Far above party level. Certain death for one or more characters unless the entire party runs for their lives!
20 Epic Violent tectonic earthquake, fire-tornado, localized orbital laser strike, or dust-tsunami. Unescapable doom. Somebody must deliberately sacrifice their life so the rest have a chance to live.
Aiding Others Instead of Taking Actions High Stakes Collaboration

During any conflict or obstacle phase, players may choose to declare support and help an ally's action rather than acting independently. The active player gains one Tactical Bonus for each aiding ally.

The Cascading Failure Warning: If the assisted player fails or busts, the consequences strike everyone involved! All helping players suffer the obstacle's full Counterattack, and the Tension Die steps up by one tier for EVERY aiding player (in addition to the tier increase from the failed active roll).
1. Bust Insurance (Reroll on Bust)
If the sequential roll exceeds 21, the active player may immediately discard the busting die and roll once more to stay in the game.
2. Threshold Reduction
Lowers the target Obstacle Difficulty Threshold by one complete step for this attempt (e.g., 20+ → 17+, or 17+ → 14+).
3. Devastating Impact (Damage Reroll)
After successfully overcoming the obstacle, the player may reroll all damage dice to maximize final damage dealt to the pool.
4. Competence Shift (Better Die)
The active player may step up their rolling die by one competence tier for this check (e.g., upgrade d20 → d12 or d10 → d8).
