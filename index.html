Why a Dedicated System?

The desolate world forged by Nuclearpasta in Ten Earth Shattering Blows is not populated by untouchable demigods or sanitized wizardry. It is a grueling, dust-choked wasteland where survivors scrape the bottom of the barrel, gambling everything for a ceramic mask or a single swallow of stale water.

Traditional tabletop engines fail to convey this visceral dread. We needed mechanics that make your palms sweat on every single die roll. A system where greed and desperation drive you past your limits... until you break.

In Blast or Bust, everything is combat and your die size shrinks as your mastery grows. The better you are, the tighter your control. But you alone decide when to hold back—or when to push your luck until you bust.

The Rule of 21

Roll your dice in sequence. Reach the Difficulty Threshold to succeed. But beware: exceed 21 and you Bust—inflicting 0 damage while suffering the world's lethal Counterattack. Rerolling all dice used to calculate damage creates a merciless push-your-luck equation of pure tension.
